=== Hattori Hanzo (ฮัตโตริ ฮันโซ) - HERO / gold ===

The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Hattori Hanzo (the legendary ninja master of Iga, a young man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: assassin (fast critical strikes). GOLD tier = HERO: a legendary human, real armour and clothes, impressive but earthly.

Face: short spiky dark hair, sharp steel-grey eyes, a cool confident half-smile. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a dark indigo ninja hood with a steel forehead plate engraved with a crest, the face mask pulled DOWN to the neck so the whole face shows.
Body: fitted dark indigo ninja garb with dark steel arm guards and shin guards, a crimson sash belt, wrapped hands, split-toe boots.
Back: a long crimson scarf flowing behind him.
Weapon: two short straight blades (tanto) in a REVERSE GRIP: a small ring or guard on top of each fist, the blade coming out of the BOTTOM of the fist and pointing back along the forearm.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a low ready stance, one blade forward at chest height, the other held back by his hip. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Hattori Hanzo at a glance: the ninja hood with the steel forehead plate and the flowing crimson scarf.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Hattori Hanzo (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): indigo #141a33 / #232c55 / #3a4880; steel #4a5060 / #8a92a6 / #d8dde8; crimson #7a1018 / #c42a30 / #ff6a5a; gold accents #c8962a / #ffd36a.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
