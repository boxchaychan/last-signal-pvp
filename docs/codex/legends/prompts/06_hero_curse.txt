=== Cleopatra (คลีโอพัตรา) - HERO / gold ===
Attach: refs/00_STYLE_wukong.png
Save as: hero_curse.png

The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Cleopatra (the last queen of ancient Egypt, a young woman) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: curse caster (poison and weakening). GOLD tier = HERO: a legendary human, real armour and clothes, impressive but earthly.

Face: a straight black bob with blunt bangs, bold kohl eyeliner, bright amber eyes, a mysterious confident smile. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a golden crown with a rearing cobra (uraeus) on the front and gold and lapis stripes.
Body: a wide gold and lapis-blue collar (usekh), a white linen dress with a gold belt, gold arm cuffs and bracelets, gold sandals.
Back: a long deep purple cape with gold edges flowing behind her.
Weapon: a golden cobra scepter: a staff topped with a rearing cobra head with glowing emerald eyes, held in her fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, an elegant regal stance, the cobra scepter held forward. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Cleopatra at a glance: the cobra crown, the black bob and the gold collar.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Cleopatra (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a; lapis blue #142a6a / #2a4aa8 / #5a82e0; white linen #d8d2c0 / #f6f2e6; purple and emerald #3a1650 / #6a2a8a / #2ad07a.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
