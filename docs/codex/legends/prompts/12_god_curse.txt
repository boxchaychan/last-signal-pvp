The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Anubis (the jackal-headed Egyptian god of the dead) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: curse caster (poison and weakening). RAINBOW tier = GOD: divine and the most impressive of all, extra gold ornament and small glowing divine details that are PART of the costume (runes, moon, sun, eyes).

Face: a black jackal head with tall pointed ears, glowing teal eyes, a calm stern look. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a gold and lapis striped headdress (nemes) behind the jackal ears.
Body: a dark-skinned body with a wide gold and lapis collar, gold arm bands, a white linen kilt with a gold belt and an ankh hanging from it, gold sandals.
Back: a long black cape with gold edges flowing behind him.
Weapon: a was-sceptre: a tall dark staff topped with a stylised jackal head and forked at the bottom, held in his fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a solemn guardian stance, the sceptre planted beside him, the free hand raised with an open palm. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Anubis at a glance: the jackal head with tall ears and the striped headdress.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Anubis (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): black #0e0e14 / #22222c / #3a3a48; gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a; lapis #142a6a / #2a4aa8 / #5a82e0; teal glow #1ac8b8 / #9affee.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
