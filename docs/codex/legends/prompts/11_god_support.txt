The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Apollo (the Greek god of the sun, music and healing, a young man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: support (heals and buffs allies). RAINBOW tier = GOD: divine and the most impressive of all, extra gold ornament and small glowing divine details that are PART of the costume (runes, moon, sun, eyes).

Face: curly golden-blond hair, warm golden eyes, a warm bright smile. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a golden laurel wreath.
Body: a white toga-style chiton with gold sun embroidery, a gold chest plate with a sun disc emblem, gold arm cuffs, laced gold sandals.
Back: a flowing orange-gold cape like sunset light.
Weapon: a golden lyre with glowing strings held in one arm, the other hand plucking it.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a cheerful stance, the lyre held up in front of him. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Apollo at a glance: the laurel wreath, the sun emblem and the golden lyre.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Apollo (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): white #d6dae6 / #ffffff; gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a; sun orange #c8501a / #ff8a2a / #ffc86a; hair #c8902a / #ffd060 / #fff0a8.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
