The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Artemis (the Greek goddess of the hunt and the moon, a young woman) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: archer (long range, piercing arrows). RAINBOW tier = GOD: divine and the most impressive of all, extra gold ornament and small glowing divine details that are PART of the costume (runes, moon, sun, eyes).

Face: long silver-white hair in a high ponytail, bright silver-blue eyes, a determined confident smile. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a silver crescent-moon tiara glowing softly on her forehead.
Body: a short white Greek chiton with silver armour pieces (chest plate with a moon emblem, shoulder guard, bracers), a silver belt, laced silver sandals up the calves.
Back: a light pale-blue cloak flowing behind her, a quiver of silver-fletched arrows on her back.
Weapon: a silver bow shaped like a crescent moon with a glowing pale-blue string, an arrow ready, held in her front fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a poised hunter stance, the bow held forward. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Artemis at a glance: the crescent-moon tiara and the crescent-moon bow.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Artemis (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): silver #6a7088 / #b8c0d6 / #f2f6ff; white #d6dae6 / #ffffff; pale blue #3a6aa8 / #7ab0e8 / #c8e6ff; moon gold #c8a24a / #ffe6a0.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
