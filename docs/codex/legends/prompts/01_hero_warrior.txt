=== Lu Bu (ลิโป้) - HERO / gold ===

The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Lu Bu (the mightiest warrior of the Three Kingdoms, a young man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: warrior (heavy hitter, smashes groups). GOLD tier = HERO: a legendary human, real armour and clothes, impressive but earthly.

Face: long black hair flowing out of the helmet, sharp red-brown eyes, a fierce proud smirk. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a tall golden crown-helmet with two VERY long pheasant tail feathers (white with dark bands) sweeping up and back, a red tassel on top.
Body: heavy dark crimson and black lamellar armour with gold trims, big shoulder guards, a gold tiger-face belt buckle, armoured boots.
Back: a long red cape and red sash ends flowing behind him.
Weapon: a huge halberd "Sky Piercer": a long red shaft, a spear tip with a crescent blade on each side, a red tassel under the blade, held in his fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a wide battle stance, the halberd held across his body ready to sweep. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Lu Bu at a glance: the two huge pheasant feathers and the crescent halberd.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Lu Bu (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): crimson #5e0f14 / #9a1c22 / #d63a3a; black armour #16131a / #2c2632 / #4a4250; gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a; feathers #f2ead8 / #6a5040.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
