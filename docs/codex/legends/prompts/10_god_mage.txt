The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Odin (the Allfather, king of the Norse gods, an old man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: mage (spells that hit areas). RAINBOW tier = GOD: divine and the most impressive of all, extra gold ornament and small glowing divine details that are PART of the costume (runes, moon, sun, eyes).

Face: a long braided white beard, an eye patch over his left eye, a bright blue right eye, a stern wise look. Both eyes bright and clearly visible (Odin's eye patch is the one exception), no hair over the eyes.
Head: a golden helmet with two white wings on the sides.
Body: steel-blue armour with gold trims and Norse knotwork, a grey wolf-fur mantle over the shoulders, a wide belt with a gold buckle, fur-topped boots; two small black ravens perched on his shoulders.
Back: a long steel-blue cloak flowing behind him.
Weapon: the spear Gungnir: a long gold-and-steel spear with glowing blue runes along the blade, held in his fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a commanding stance, the spear held upright, the free hand raised with glowing runes floating close to the palm. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Odin at a glance: the winged helmet, the eye patch, the ravens and the rune spear.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Odin (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): steel blue #1a2a44 / #34507a / #6a8ab8; grey fur #5a5a62 / #9a9aa6 / #d6d6de; gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a; rune glow #3ac8ff / #c8f2ff.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
