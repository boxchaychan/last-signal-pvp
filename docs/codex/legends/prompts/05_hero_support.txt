The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Zhuge Liang (Kongming) (the genius strategist of the Three Kingdoms, a young man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: support (heals and buffs allies). GOLD tier = HERO: a legendary human, real armour and clothes, impressive but earthly.

Face: long straight black hair, calm dark eyes, a calm gentle smile. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a black scholar's cap (guan) tied with ribbons.
Body: flowing white and sky-blue Han scholar robes with cloud patterns and gold trims, wide sleeves, a black sash with a jade pendant, cloth shoes.
Back: long white robe tails and blue ribbons flowing behind him.
Weapon: the iconic white crane-feather fan held up in his fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a relaxed elegant stance, the feather fan raised in front of him, the other hand behind his back. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Zhuge Liang (Kongming) at a glance: the white feather fan and the black scholar cap.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Zhuge Liang (Kongming) (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): white robe #c8ccd8 / #e8ecf4 / #ffffff; sky blue #2a5a8a / #4a8ac8 / #9ad0ff; black #141418 / #2a2a33; gold and jade #c8962a / #ffd36a / #3aa87a.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
