The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Loki (the Norse trickster god born of fire, a young man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: assassin (fast critical strikes). RAINBOW tier = GOD: divine and the most impressive of all, extra gold ornament and small glowing divine details that are PART of the costume (runes, moon, sun, eyes).

Face: wild flame-red hair, sly emerald-green eyes, a sly mischievous grin. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a black leather hood with a serpent-scale pattern pushed back so the flame-red hair spills out, a thin gold circlet shaped like a snake biting its tail on the forehead.
Body: black and deep purple leather armour with gold serpent knotwork, a high collar, gold bracers shaped like coiled snakes, slim black boots; faint glowing emerald runes on the armour.
Back: a long dark purple cape with a ragged edge and an emerald lining flowing behind him.
Weapon: two curved daggers shaped like white serpent fangs with gold hilts, in a REVERSE GRIP: a ring on top of each fist, the blade coming out of the BOTTOM of the fist and curving back toward the body.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a low sly stance, one fang dagger forward, the other held back. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Loki at a glance: the flame-red hair under the serpent hood and the fang daggers.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Loki (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): black and purple #121216 / #2a1a3a / #4a2a6a; flame hair #8a1a0a / #e0441a / #ffa040; gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a; emerald #0e5a3a / #1ab874 / #8affc8.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
