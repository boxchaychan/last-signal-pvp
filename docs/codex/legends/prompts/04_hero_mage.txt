The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Merlin (the great wizard of King Arthur's court, a cheerful old man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: mage (spells that hit areas). GOLD tier = HERO: a legendary human, real armour and clothes, impressive but earthly.

Face: a long fluffy white beard, bushy white eyebrows, bright blue eyes, a wise knowing smile. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a tall floppy pointed wizard hat, deep blue with silver stars and crescent moons.
Body: a deep blue wizard robe with silver star embroidery and a wide belt with a silver moon buckle, wide sleeves, curled pointed shoes.
Back: a long midnight-blue cloak with silver edges flowing behind him.
Weapon: a tall gnarled wooden staff topped with a big glowing cyan crystal held in its roots, gripped in his fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, standing proudly, the staff planted beside him, the free hand raised with fingers open as if casting. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Merlin at a glance: the starry pointed hat, the long white beard and the crystal staff.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Merlin (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): royal blue #141c4a / #24357a / #3f5cbc; silver #8a90a8 / #d6dbe8 / #ffffff; beard #d8d6e0 / #ffffff; crystal #1a9ab8 / #5ae0ff / #d6faff.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
