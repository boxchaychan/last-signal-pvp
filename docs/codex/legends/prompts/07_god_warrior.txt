The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. This IS the character in the style example: keep his design, colours and staff exactly, only redraw him STANDING on the ground in a battle stance.

Draw Sun Wukong (the Monkey King, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: warrior (heavy hitter, smashes groups). RAINBOW tier = GOD: divine and the most impressive of all, extra gold ornament and small glowing divine details that are PART of the costume (runes, moon, sun, eyes).

Face: a monkey face with golden-brown fur, wild spiky golden-brown hair, fierce red eyes, round monkey ears, a fierce toothy grin. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a golden circlet with cloud swirls on his forehead (exactly like the style example).
Body: black and gold armour with a gold dragon shoulder guard, a red rope sash with white fur trim, black baggy trousers, black and gold boots.
Back: long red scarves with gold cloud patterns flowing behind him, a curling monkey tail.
Weapon: the Ruyi Jingu Bang: a red staff with ornate gold ends, resting on his shoulder in his fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a wide battle stance, the staff on his shoulder, the free hand open like a claw (same character as the example, now standing on the ground). Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Sun Wukong at a glance: the golden circlet, the monkey tail and the red-and-gold staff.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Sun Wukong (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): fur #6a3a1a / #a8642a / #e0a050; red #7a1018 / #c42a30 / #ff6a5a; black #16131a / #2c2632; gold #8a5a12 / #d8a22a / #ffe07a.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
