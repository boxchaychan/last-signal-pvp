=== Robin Hood (โรบินฮู้ด) - HERO / gold ===
Attach: refs/00_STYLE_wukong.png

The attached picture is the STYLE EXAMPLE (a chibi Sun Wukong). Copy its art style exactly: chibi proportions with a big head (about 40-45% of the height), crisp clean pixel art on one even grid, a bold dark outline, rich clean shading, smooth tidy shapes with no noise, lots of fine gold ornament, cloth and scarves that flow with motion, a fierce expressive face with big bright eyes, plain white background. Copy only the style of the example, NOT its monkey features, colours or staff.

Draw Robin Hood (the outlaw archer of Sherwood Forest, a young man) as a chibi pixel-art game character. Class: archer (long range, piercing arrows). GOLD tier = HERO: a legendary human, real armour and clothes, impressive but earthly.

Face: messy auburn hair, bright green eyes, a cheeky confident grin. Both eyes bright and clearly visible, no hair over the eyes.
Head: a forest-green hooded cap with a long red feather tucked in it.
Body: a brown leather jerkin over a green tunic, leather bracers with gold rims, a belt with pouches and a gold buckle, a quiver of arrows on his back, soft leather boots.
Back: a short forest-green hooded cape with a ragged edge flowing behind him.
Weapon: a tall yew longbow with green wraps on the grip, an arrow ready on the string, held in his front fist.
Pose: three-quarter view facing RIGHT, a confident stance, the bow held forward and slightly raised. Both feet on the ground (the characters stand on tiles in the game), with the energy and flowing cloth of the example.
Must read as Robin Hood at a glance: the green feathered cap, the longbow and the quiver.
Original design: do not copy any existing game, film, anime or comic version of Robin Hood (for example no Marvel, Dragon Ball or Black Myth look).
Colours (dark / mid / light): forest green #173a1e / #2e6a32 / #5aa24e; leather #3a2414 / #6e4628 / #a87448; red feather #a01e1e / #e04a3a; gold #b8862a / #ffd36a.

Square canvas, plain pure white background with nothing else, the character centred, full body from the top of the head gear to the feet, filling about 85-90% of the height. No aura or glow round the body, no effects, no sparkles, no ground, no shadow, no text, no watermark.
