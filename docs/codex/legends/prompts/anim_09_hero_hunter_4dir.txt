=== Robin Hood (โรบินฮู้ด) animation, south/east/north (west = mirrored east) ===
Attach: art/incoming/legends/hero_hunter.png

Use $game-character-sprites.
Reference: the attached picture (chibi Robin Hood with the longbow and the red-feathered cap). It is the final design: keep the exact same character, face, hair, outfit, colours, weapon and level of detail in every frame.
Scope: 3 directions, in this order: south (facing the viewer), east (facing right, the same three-quarter view as the reference), north (back to the viewer). Do NOT make west: the game mirrors east to get west. Actions for EACH direction: idle (4 frames), attack (6 frames) and skill (6 frames). No walk. Cell 128x128 for the clean sheets.
The reference shows the east view. For south and north, turn the same character to face that way and keep every part of the costume and weapon the same. The steps below are the same beats in every direction, aimed the way that direction faces (south = toward the viewer, north = away from the viewer, east = right).

Idle (4 frames, a smooth loop that ends where it starts): confident archer stance, bow held ready, the red feather and green cape sway.

Attack (6 frames) - piercing arrow:
  1. stance, an arrow nocked
  2. draw the string
  3. full draw aiming straight ahead
  4. release (the arrow is gone), a cheeky grin
  5. follow-through
  6. take a new arrow, back to the stance

Skill (6 frames) - Sherwood Arrow Rain (ฝนธนูเชอร์วูด), the ultimate (in the game it rains 50 arrows over the whole field):
  1. stance
  2. grab a handful of arrows from the quiver
  3. nock several arrows at once, aiming high
  4. full draw upward
  5. release upward (the arrows are gone), laughing
  6. back to the stance

Do not draw flying arrows: the game draws the shots itself.
SAME SCALE IN EVERY ROW AND DIRECTION: the character is exactly the same height in idle, attack and skill in south, east and north (do not draw the idle rows bigger), the same as in the reference.
Smooth animation: every frame is a small step from the one before; the same size, proportions and outline in every frame; both feet on the same ground line unless a step says jump; hair, cape and cloth follow through and settle.
Keep the whole body, weapon and cape inside every frame. No detached effects (no flying projectiles, slash trails, magic bolts, sparks, dust or shadows): the game draws those. Parts of the costume or weapon that already glow may glow brighter. Transparent background.
LAYOUT (owner): never put all frames in one long row. Draw one action per row (idle row, attack row, skill row; one block of three rows per direction), every frame in its own equal-size square cell with empty space all around the figure; no part of a frame (body, weapon, cape) may touch or cross into the next cell. Use this layout in the generated images and in the sheets.
When done, zip the whole run folder including the full-size raw images in source/ and generated/, the clean sheets and the GIF previews, plus one large preview picture of all nine rows. Do not shrink anything.
