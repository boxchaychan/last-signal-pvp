## Style (all pictures)
Smooth 2D cartoon for a cute mobile game. NOT pixel art, no pixel grid, no 3D render, no painterly noise.
- Characters: chibi, about 2.2 heads tall, big head, big sparkling eyes, small body, thick dark outline (dark navy-brown), flat bright
  colours with simple soft cel shading (one shadow tone, one small highlight), clean vector-like shapes that still read at 60 px tall.
- Original designs only: no characters, logos or UI from any existing game.
- No text, no watermark, no ground shadow under characters, no frames or borders round the cells.
- Two reference pictures are attached. `heroes_5.png` = our own earlier pixel versions of five of the heroes: keep their identities
  (hair, outfit colours, weapon) but REDRAW them in the smooth 2D style above. `look_v2c.png` = the screen layout of the game (pond in the
  middle, brick walkway ring round it): use it for composition only, not for style.

## 1. `heroes_a.png`, 1536 x 1024, transparent background
(if a transparent background is not possible, use one flat pure magenta #FF00FF background)
Six heroes in a 3 x 2 grid of equal cells (512 x 512 each), one hero centred in each cell. IMPORTANT: all six at the SAME scale and the
same body height, about 70 % of the cell height, feet on the same line in each row, nothing touching the cell edges. Each stands facing the
viewer, turned slightly to the right, relaxed ready pose, holding the weapon.
1. Recruit swordsman boy: messy brown hair, green tunic, leather belt, short steel sword, round wooden shield.
2. Archer girl: blonde ponytail, sky-blue hooded cape, wooden bow with an arrow.
3. Ice mage girl: short white hair, deep-blue robe and pointed hat with a snowflake emblem, staff with a glowing ice crystal.
4. Fire wizard boy: spiky red hair, crimson robe with gold trim, a small flame floating above his open hand.
5. Shadow ninja: dark purple outfit, long red scarf, masked lower face, a kunai in each hand.
6. Golden paladin: shining gold armour, white cape, winged helmet with the FACE VISIBLE, glowing golden greatsword held upright.

## 2. `monsters_a.png`, 1536 x 1024, transparent background (or flat #FF00FF)
Six small monsters in a 3 x 2 grid of equal cells, same rules. All in SIDE view facing LEFT, in a walking pose. They are SMALLER than the
heroes: each about 50 % of the cell height (the heroes were 70 %). Cute but a little mean.
1. Leaf slime: round green jelly with a leaf sprout on top.
2. Mushroom critter: red cap with white spots, stubby cream body, angry brows.
3. Goblin: green skin, big ears, red bandana, wooden club.
4. Wolf pup: grey fur, white chest, yellow eyes.
5. Lantern ghost: pale teal ghost with a wispy tail, holding a small blue lantern.
6. Skeleton soldier: big skull, ragged purple cape, rusty sword, small round shield.

## 3. `boss_a.png`, 1024 x 1024, transparent background (or flat #FF00FF)
One boss: the Mushroom King. Huge purple-red cap with white spots and a golden crown, big angry face, fat cream body, thick arms and fists,
mossy patches. Side three-quarter view facing LEFT, walking. Fills about 85 % of the picture.

## 4. `arena_a.png`, 1024 x 1536 (portrait), NOT transparent
The play field seen from above (top-down, camera slightly tilted like a board game). A rectangular lotus pond of calm turquoise water in the
middle, surrounded on all four sides by a wide, EVEN walkway of warm sandstone bricks: the walkway is about 11 % of the picture width on the
left and on the right and about 9 % of the picture height at the top and at the bottom, with rounded outer corners. A thin band of grass
with tiny flowers outside the walkway. IMPORTANT: the walkway must be clean and empty (monsters walk on it): no plants, rocks or objects on
it. The pond interior must be mostly EMPTY calm water (the game draws two boards there): only gentle ripples, soft sparkles and a few lily
pads with pink lotus flowers close to the pond's edges. Bright, cheerful, sunny. No characters, no text, no UI.

## 5. `icons_a.png`, 1024 x 1024, transparent background (or flat #FF00FF)
Eight game icons in a 4 x 2 grid of equal cells, one centred in each cell, filling about 75 % of it, same thick outline and flat bright
colours: gold coin, four-leaf clover, white skull, red heart, crossed swords, rolled scroll with a red seal, glowing blue summoning gem,
round stopwatch.
