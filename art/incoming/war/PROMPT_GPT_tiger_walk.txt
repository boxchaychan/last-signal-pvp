8 bit pixel art game sprite sheet. The FIRST picture is a motion reference: 8 numbered cells in a 4 x 2 grid showing a RUN cycle of another character. It is a loop: after cell 8 comes cell 1 again. Use it ONLY for the poses (body lean, arm and leg positions, which foot is on the ground). Do not copy that character's look.

The SECOND picture is the character to draw: the 8 bit TIGER warrior. Draw THIS tiger once in every cell, running, in the same pose as the reference figure of that cell.

Rules:
- The same tiger in all 8 cells: same face, colours, black hood, white fur collar, red sash, claws, striped tail, same pixel size and proportions as the second picture, the SAME SIZE in every cell.
- View: the same as the second picture, three-quarter view, body facing right and toward the viewer, seen a little from above. The tail swings with the run.
- Keep the 4 x 2 grid and the order 1 to 8 (top row left to right, then the bottom row). One figure per cell, feet on the same ground line, nothing crossing into another cell.
- Do NOT draw numbers, cell borders or a ground line. No motion lines, no dust, no effects, no shadow, no text.
- Chunky bold pixels, thick black outline, same 8 bit style as the second picture. Plain flat pure white background. Landscape picture, 1536 x 1024.
