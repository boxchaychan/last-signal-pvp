Pixel art character sheet for a mobile action game. Tiny black stick-figure champions, each about 32 pixels tall, drawn on a clear visible pixel grid (every art pixel a big crisp square, no blur, no anti-aliasing, no gradients).

Style: the whole body is solid pure black: a round head, thin stick limbs 2 pixels thick, small round hands and feet. NO face except two small glowing eyes. The ONLY colour on each character is a LONG flowing scarf around the neck whose two ends trail far behind in the wind (as long as the body or longer, waving shape), plus the glowing eyes in the same colour.

Draw 5 animal tribe champions in ONE row, evenly spaced, same height, side view facing RIGHT, standing ready pose:
1. Rabbit: two long upright ears on the head. Scarf and eyes: white with a light blue shade.
2. Bear: two small round ears, a slightly thicker and wider body. Scarf and eyes: amber yellow.
3. Fox: two pointed ears and a big bushy black tail. Scarf and eyes: red.
4. Cat: two small triangle ears and a thin long curling tail. Scarf and eyes: purple.
5. Eagle: no ears, a hooked beak on the head and two small black wings folded on the back. Scarf and eyes: cyan blue.
They must be easy to tell apart by the black shape alone (ears, tail, beak).

Hands EMPTY, no weapons (weapons are added later in the game). The front hand held a little forward so it is clearly visible.

Second row underneath: the same rabbit in 4 action poses with the scarf flowing: run, jump, a big forward slash motion with the empty hand, and a hit / knocked back pose.

Plain flat single-colour background (#d9d4c7 light parchment, so the black bodies stand out), no scenery, no text, no labels.
