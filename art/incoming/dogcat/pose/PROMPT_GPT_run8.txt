The FIRST picture is a pose guide: 8 numbered cells in a 4 x 2 grid, each with a colour-coded stick figure doing one step of a RUN cycle (a loop: after cell 8 comes cell 1 again), seen from the side, facing RIGHT (the small point on the yellow head is the nose). Colour key: yellow = head, blue = body, red + orange = the FRONT arm (upper arm, forearm) , purple + pink = the back arm, green + light green = the front leg, teal + light blue = the back leg. The grey line under each figure is the ground.

The SECOND picture is the character. Draw THIS character once in every cell, in exactly the pose of that cell's stick figure: the same body lean, the same arm and leg angles, the feet where the stick figure has them (a foot that is in the air stays in the air).

Rules:
- The same character in all 8 cells: same face, colours, outfit and proportions as the second picture, the SAME SIZE in every cell (about as tall as the stick figure), seen from the side facing right.
- Keep the 4 x 2 grid and the order 1 to 8 (top row left to right, then the bottom row). One figure per cell, centred like the guide, nothing crossing into another cell.
- Do NOT draw the stick figures, the colours, the numbers, the cell borders or the ground line in the result. No motion lines, no swoosh, no dust, no effects, no shadow, no text.
- Same art style as the second picture, clean outlines, flat colours with simple shading.
- Plain flat pure white background. Landscape picture, 1536 x 1024.
- If the character carries a weapon, it stays in the same hand in the same carrying position in every cell (for the shiba: the katana resting on his shoulder).
