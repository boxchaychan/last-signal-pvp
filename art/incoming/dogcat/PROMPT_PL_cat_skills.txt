ทุกท่าเริ่มและจบที่ท่ายืน เพื่อให้ต่อกับท่ายืนในเกมได้เนียน

==== 1 ดาว: "ปลาแห้งสามตัวรวด" (Fish Barrage) ====
ปาปลาแห้ง 3 ตัวติดกันเร็ว ๆ ซ้าย ขวา ซ้าย แล้วทำท่าอวด

smug cat ninja skill: he quickly pulls three dried fish from his belt and throws them one after another in a fast rapid combo, left paw, right paw, left paw, his body twisting hard with each throw, then he dusts off his paws and strikes a proud pose before going back to his smug standing pose; keep the same south-east diagonal facing in every frame, do not turn toward the camera

==== 2 ดาว: "ม้วนตัวปาดาวกระจาย" (Spinning Star Throw) ====
กระโดดขึ้น ม้วนตัวกลางอากาศ ปาดาวกระจายลงพื้นเป็นวงพัด แล้วลงพื้นแบบเท่ ๆ

smug cat ninja skill: he crouches low, jumps high into the air, spins once in a tight somersault with his scarf whirling, and at the top of the jump throws a fan of fish-skeleton shurikens down and forward with both paws, then lands heavily on one knee with a hard impact and stands back up into his smug pose; keep the same south-east diagonal facing in every frame, do not turn toward the camera

==== 3 ดาว: "พายุปลาทองคำ" (Golden Fish Storm) ====
ง้างดาวกระจายทองยักษ์ด้วยสองมือ หมุนตัวเหวี่ยงปาสุดแรง แล้วยืดอกโพสท่าสุดขี้อวด

smug cat ninja master skill: he raises the big glowing golden fish-skeleton shuriken high above his head with both paws, it charges up and glows brighter, he spins his whole body around once to build power with his long scarf whipping around him, then hurls the shuriken forward with a huge powerful throw that pushes his body forward, then he catches it again as it flies back and strikes a very smug victory pose before returning to his standing pose; keep the same south-east diagonal facing in every frame, do not turn toward the camera
