Copy his design exactly from this sheet: the swept-back tufted topknot tied with red, orange fur, cream face and chest, half-closed calm eyes, dark navy robe with the pale pattern near the hem, red sash, curled tail, short legs, small chubby body, the katana carried on his back. Do not change his proportions, do not make him taller or bigger, do not redesign the topknot, do not add a sheath at the hip.

Make ONE picture: a pixel-art sprite sheet of this character (a small chubby samurai shiba dog standing on two legs). Keep him exactly the same in every frame: same orange fur, cream face, topknot with a red tie, dark navy robe with a red sash, curled tail, same proportions, the SAME SIZE in every frame, same chunky pixel-art style. Side view, facing RIGHT in every frame.

Layout: a grid of 4 columns x 2 rows = 8 frames, read left to right, top row first. Every frame is its own equal square cell with empty space around the figure; nothing touches or crosses into another cell. Plain flat bright magenta background (#FF00FF) everywhere, no grid lines, no numbers, no text, no floor, no shadow, no enemy.
Do not draw slash trails, swooshes, sparks, dust or glow: only the character and his katana. No breath puff, no line under the feet.
Every frame must be a clearly DIFFERENT pose, each one a small step after the previous, like frames of a smooth animation.

The 8 frames show ONE move, hit 1 of a sword combo: a horizontal slash.
1 standing ready, hand going to the katana handle behind his shoulder. 2 drawing the katana out. 3 katana pulled far back behind him at shoulder height, body twisted back. 4 starting the swing, stepping forward. 5 mid swing, katana level, pointing forward. 6 full reach, arm and katana stretched straight ahead (the hit). 7 follow-through, katana past his front, body leaning forward. 8 katana held low in front of him, knees bent.
