## Style (all 6 sheets)
The attached picture `ref_icons.png` is our own item icon sheet: it is THE style. Draw NEW icons in exactly that style:
detailed dark-fantasy pixel-art game icons, thick dark outline, rich shading, bright glowing magic accents, each icon ONE object seen
from the front at a slight angle, the object filling most of its cell like the icons of the reference.
- Every sheet = ONE picture, 1536 x 1024, a grid of 4 columns x 3 rows = 12 icons, every icon centred in its own equal cell with
  clear empty space all round it (an icon must never touch or cross into a neighbouring cell).
- TRANSPARENT background. If a transparent background is not possible, use ONE flat pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere instead.
- No grid lines, no frames, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no shadows on the background.
- Original designs only: nothing copied from an existing game.
- Order in every sheet: left to right, top to bottom, exactly as numbered below (the game cuts the icons by position).
- Before saving a sheet, look at it: 12 icons, 4 x 3, right order, nothing cut off. Redo a sheet that fails.
