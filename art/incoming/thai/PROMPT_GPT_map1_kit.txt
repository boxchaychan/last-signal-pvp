กติกาทุกชีท (ใส่ท้ายทุกครั้ง):
Same painted 2D style and colours as the attached overview. Side view, camera straight from the side. Flat pure green background #00B140 behind every piece, nothing touching the edges, wide gaps between pieces, no shadows on the background, no text, no characters.

ชีท 1 — แท่นหิน / ขอบชั้น (ชิ้นหลักของเลโก้)
Paint a game asset sheet of platform pieces for a 2D side-scroller, laterite brick and rock with a bright grass top edge, 6 pieces in 2 rows:
row 1: LEFT end cap, MIDDLE piece that tiles seamlessly left-right (its left and right edges must match exactly), RIGHT end cap — each 1 tile tall (grass top + about 1 brick course).
row 2: a tall solid wall block that tiles seamlessly up-down AND left-right (just bricks and rock, no grass), the same wall with roots and vines, a corner piece where a grass edge meets a wall going down.

ชีท 2 — ทางขึ้นลง
Paint a game asset sheet: a rope ladder (top hook piece, a middle piece that tiles up-down, a bottom piece), an old brick stairway going up to the right, the same going up to the left, a hanging vine curtain that tiles up-down.

ชีท 3 — ประตูวาร์ปและของบนยอด
Paint a game asset sheet: a glowing warp gate shaped like a small Thai temple arch (gold frame, swirling violet-blue portal), a big ruined prang tower for the summit, a ruined bell-shaped chedi, a broken temple wall piece, a stone lantern.

ชีท 4 — ของประกอบฉากชั้นล่าง
Paint a game asset sheet: a Thai sala pavilion (red-orange tiered roof, gold chofa), two clay water jars, a coconut palm, a sugar palm, a banana plant, a small campfire ring, a lotus pond edge piece.

ชีท 5 — ของประกอบฉากชั้นกลาง/บน
Paint a game asset sheet: leaning old grave stones (3 kinds), a huge rain tree with hanging roots, a moss-covered Buddha-less stone plinth, tree roots growing over bricks, small grass tufts and flowers (5 kinds), floating blue will-o'-wisp flame.

ชีท 6 — ฉากหลังไกล (ซ้อนชั้นได้)
Paint 3 wide background strips that tile left-right seamlessly: (1) far hazy violet temple spires and hills against the sunset, (2) a nearer row of dark palms and rooftops, (3) a sunset sky with clouds, a big low sun and floating khom loi lanterns (this one can stay on the sky colour, no green background).
