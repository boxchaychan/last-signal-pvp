==== ก๊อปส่ง (รูปที่ 1 ซ้าย) ====
Paint ONE very wide side-view concept of a 2D side-scrolling MMO grinding map, the widest landscape size you can make. This is the LEFT third of a long map; later pictures will continue it to the right, so the right edge must be able to continue (no ending, no frame).
STYLE: exactly the same line style as the attached archer girl: bold clean dark outlines on everything, bright saturated colours, crisp cel shading with 2-3 tones, small white / light-blue / gold highlights, smooth polished 2D like a Korean side-scroller MMO. NOT pixel art, NOT 3D, NOT a soft painterly blur, NOT a photo. The archer must look like she belongs in this world.
THEME: an old Thai floating market at dusk turning to night, lightly haunted but cosy and beautiful: wooden stilt houses with steep Thai roofs, canals, rowing boats piled with fruit and flowers, paper lanterns and hanging lights, lotus, palms, misty water, faint blue ghost lights. Not scary, not gory.
LAYOUT (the real map, camera straight from the side): 5 walkable floors stacked like a wide market town on stilts, every floor runs the FULL WIDTH of the picture, nothing floats in the sky:
- floor 1 (bottom): a long wooden boardwalk along the canal, the water below it at the very bottom.
- floors 2-4: long wooden decks / balconies of the stilt houses and market halls, the space between two floors is the wall / front of the building of the floor above (wood planks, shutters, shop fronts), set back slightly so it reads as background.
- floor 5 (top): the roofs and a rooftop terrace with the sunset sky above.
- wooden ladders and wooden stairs connect the floors, alternating left and right, plus a few gaps you can drop through.
- the left end: a small arrival dock with a glowing Thai temple-arch warp gate (where the player comes in from map 1).
Clear flat walking surfaces (a clean top edge on every floor). No characters, no monsters, no text, no UI, no frame or border.
