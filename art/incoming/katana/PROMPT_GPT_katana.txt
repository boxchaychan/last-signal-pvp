DESIGN (what makes it read at ~96 px):
- Body: slim and tall, about 6 heads (not chibi), long legs, standing in a low sword stance: feet wide apart, knees bent, body turned three-quarter to the right.
- Colours: almost two-tone like ink: cream-white and near-black, plus ONE accent colour = crimson red. Max ~16 colours in the whole sprite.
  Cream: hair, inner kimono, bandaged forearms. Black: short haori jacket, hakama trousers, boots. Crimson: sash, the cord of the hair tie, the glow on the blade edge.
- Head: white hair tied high in a long ponytail that flows behind (a clear shape in the silhouette), narrow eyes, small face.
- Katana: long and thin, held low with both hands, blade pointing down-forward diagonally away from the body (never across the body),
  silver blade with a thin crimson edge glow, black scabbard on the left hip, small round guard.
- Silhouette: arms, sword, ponytail and haori tails all stick OUT of the body so the black shape alone reads as "swordsman in stance".
- Light from the top left: every part has one lit side and one shadow side (2-3 tones each), no gradients, no soft shading.
