Character design sheet for a mobile 2D side-view action game: "Mecha Beast Champions", tiny armored robot animal warriors, each with a strong personality that shows in the pose, the body language, the eyes and small details.

STYLE (use the attached picture ONLY as a style reference, do NOT copy its character): dark fantasy mecha chibi, bold black ink outlines, glossy black armor plates with sharp spikes and edges, strong rim light, glowing neon accents (eyes, energy cores, weapon edges), a little torn cloth (scarf, bandage or banner) flowing behind. Polished game character art, NOT pixel art.

PROPORTIONS: chibi, about 2 heads tall: big helmet-head, short stubby body and legs, small chunky hands and clawed feet.

Draw 5 champions in ONE row, all the same height, each seen from the SIDE facing RIGHT, in a pose that shows who they are:

1. ZIP, the Rabbit mech (cyan): hyperactive speedster, cocky and playful. Bouncing on her toes, leaning forward, one twin energy dagger spinning on a finger, the long armored ears perked up like antenna blades, a big grin-shaped visor glow, a cyan scarf whipping behind. Sticker-like scratches and a racing number painted on her armor.

5. VOLT, the Eagle mech (violet): proud, noble commander. Standing very straight, chin up, wings folded like a cape, the energy spear planted upright like a flag, a hooked beak visor with sharp focused eyes, a small violet banner on the back with a feather emblem.

Each must be recognisable by its silhouette alone (ears, tail, beak, wings, weapon, pose).

Under each character, small: nothing. No text, no names, no labels. Plain flat pure white background, every character complete with space around it, no ground shadows, no scenery.
